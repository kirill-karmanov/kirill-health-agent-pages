Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This policy explains how Kirill Health Agent, a personal and non-commercial wellness assistant, handles information obtained through Google Health API authorization.

1. Who uses the application

The application is intended for use only by its owner. It is not offered as a public service and does not support advertising, data brokerage, or third-party user accounts.

2. Information accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, the application may read Google Health profile and settings information, activity and fitness data, sleep data, health metrics and measurements, and nutrition data. Requested Google Health permissions are read-only.

3. Purpose of processing

Information is used only to produce private wellness summaries, identify personal trends, support exercise and recovery planning, and answer the owner's questions. The application is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease.

4. Storage and AI processing

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on a user-controlled server with restricted file permissions.
  • Tokens and client secrets are not included in prompts, reports, or public pages.
  • Health metrics and derived summaries may be stored locally in agent sessions, scheduled-report output, and personal tracking files until deleted by the owner.
  • When the configured AI model is used to generate a summary, the minimum relevant health context may be processed by that model provider under the provider's applicable terms and privacy controls.

5. Sharing and sale

Personal health information is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally disclosed to unrelated third parties. Data is exchanged only with Google APIs, the owner's user-controlled system, and the configured AI service when needed to fulfill the owner's request.

6. Retention and deletion

The owner controls retention. Local tokens, cached data, session history, and generated reports can be deleted at any time. Google access can be revoked from the Google Account permissions page; revocation prevents future API access.

7. Security

Reasonable safeguards include read-only scopes, local credential storage, restrictive token-file permissions, redaction of secrets, and limited access to the host system. No method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's data access or processing changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be submitted through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/kirill-karmanov.
